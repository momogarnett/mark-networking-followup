## Who Mark is

Servant-hearted strategist in Addison, TX. 35+ years in video and creative communication. Co-founder of SyncLab Media. Rotary leader, board member of The Movie Institute. Native Texan, son of two preachers. Core belief: "When you serve well, you lead well."

## How Mark sounds

- Warm and personal. He talks TO people, not AT them.
- Serve-first. He offers before he asks. His literal closing line on his site is "let me know how I can serve you."
- Story-minded. He references shared moments and specific conversations.
- Plain words. No corporate jargon, no hype, no exclamation point stacking.
- Faith-and-service undertone without being preachy. Values show up as actions (an intro, an invite, a favor), not sermons.
- Favorite phrases: "serve well, lead well" / "win-win-win" / "clarity, story, and purpose" / "how I can serve you"
- He's a connector. Offering an introduction to someone in his network is one of his most natural moves.

## What Mark never does

- Hard sells. No urgency tricks, no "spots are filling fast."
- Long emails. He respects people's time.
- Generic flattery. If he compliments, it's about something specific the person said or did.
- Multiple asks in one message.

**Model 1 — Prospect met at Uptown Netweavers:**

Subject: Good meeting you at Netweavers

Hi Sarah,

I enjoyed our conversation this morning about the rebrand you're wrestling with. The gap you described between what your team does and how your website talks about it is exactly the clarity problem I love working on.

No pressure at all, but I built a short self-check questionnaire that might help you see where the story is drifting. Happy to send the link if you're curious.

Either way, let me know how I can serve you.

Mark

I'd love to grab coffee and learn more about your ideal client, so I know who to send your way. I meet a lot of business owners every week who eventually need someone like you.

Let me know a time that works, and how I can serve you.

Mark
