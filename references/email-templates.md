# Follow-Up Email Templates

These are shapes, not scripts. Always rewrite around the specific thing the person and Mark talked about. Replace everything in [brackets].

---

## Prospect

Subject: Great meeting you at [event]

Hi [Name],

Really enjoyed our conversation about [specific topic] at [event]. [One sentence reacting to their situation, showing he listened.]

One quick thought: [one genuinely useful idea related to their challenge].

If it would help, I put together a short self-check that shows how clearly a business is communicating its purpose. Happy to send you the link, or we can just grab coffee and talk it through.

Let me know how I can serve you.

Mark
